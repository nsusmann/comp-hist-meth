Spatial analysis

Churches of Patmos

Compare the same locations through three visual encodings. Map positions use teaching-level precision; coordinate values are not displayed.

As a researcher, you need to decide whether a graphical or spatial output makes sense. For the purposes of my project, the end result will probably be a static map presented in the actual book. Whether or not I choose to make this interactive map (or some other iteration) available to the public depends on a number of questions: Is there a large enough audience? Do I have the capacity to maintain this digital asset? Could I better serve the community with some other format, like a pamphlet available at the local cultural heritage center?
